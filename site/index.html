[[what-you-get]]

The part you dread
is the part it does.

  • You stop scrolling back.

    Nobody rereads a three-week thread for fun. Minne has already been there, so the context you would have gone digging for is in the first draft.

  • It sounds like you.

    It learns your tone from your own writing — short or warm, English or Norwegian, sign-off and all. Drafts come out sounding like a person who was paying attention.

  • Five minutes becomes fifteen seconds.

    That is one reply. Do the arithmetic on a working week of them and you get your Friday afternoon back.

  • Nothing to keep up.

    No tagging, no notebooks, no “capture this”. Minne watches the work you were doing anyway and remembers it for you.

[[your-mac-only]]

A memory this good
should only be yours.

Minne has no account and no server. What it remembers is a folder of plain notes on your Mac — open them, edit them, delete them, take them with you.

  • Nothing is stored anywhere but your Mac. No sign-up, no tracking, no cloud copy.
  • It reads the words in the window you are working in — never a picture of your screen.
  • Card numbers and ID numbers are blanked out before anything is saved, and password fields are skipped entirely.
  • One button erases every last note, for good.
  • It runs on the AI you already pay for — Claude, ChatGPT, or a model on your own machine.

[[what-leaves]]

What leaves your Mac, exactly

The only network traffic Minne makes is requests to the AI provider you signed in with — your account, your key. There is no Minne server to talk to, and no telemetry. This is what each feature sends that provider, and nothing else goes anywhere:

draft
the field you are writing in, the window around it, and the notes Minne recalls — one model request.
chat
your question, and the notes the answer needs.
sync
recent captures, in batches, already masked, to be distilled into notes.
sign-in
one OAuth exchange with that same provider. An API key stays on your Mac and is shown to no one else.
never
the folder itself. No backup, no cloud sync, no upload path in the code.

Want even that to stay home? Point Minne at Ollama on your own machine and model traffic never leaves it.
