Nothing is typed into the app until you press Insert — and ⌘Z takes it straight back out. The same key, the same panel, in every app on your Mac.

[[what-it-remembers]]

It watched the work.
Now it's a folder of notes.

While you work, Minne reads the words in the window in front of you — never a picture of your screen — and keeps what matters as plain notes in ~/Minne. People, projects, decisions, days. That folder is what the drafts are written from.

  • Plain markdown files on your Mac. Open them, edit them, delete them, take them with you.
  • No tagging, no “capture this”. It remembers the work you were doing anyway.
  • Nothing is stored anywhere but your Mac — no account, no server, no cloud copy.

[[the-facts]]

The short version.

Price
Free. MIT licensed, and the source is yours to read.
Runs on
macOS 14 and later. A menu-bar app, no window to keep open.
The key
Right-Option, in any text field. ⌥ Space opens a chat with your memory.
The AI
The one you already pay for — Claude or ChatGPT with your own sign-in — or Ollama on your machine.
Your data
Stays on your Mac. No account, no Minne server, no telemetry. Card and ID numbers are blanked before anything is saved; password fields are skipped.
Undo
Nothing is inserted until you press Insert. One button erases every note, for good.
What leaves your Mac, exactly

The only network traffic Minne makes is requests to the AI provider you signed in with — your account, your key — plus one anonymous version check a day against GitHub, which you can turn off. There is no Minne server to talk to, and no telemetry. This is what each feature sends, and nothing else goes anywhere:

draft
the field you are writing in, the window around it, and the notes Minne recalls — one model request.
chat
your question, and the notes the answer needs.
sync
recent captures, in batches, already masked, to be distilled into notes.
sign-in
one OAuth exchange with that same provider. An API key stays on your Mac and is shown to no one else.
updates
once a day, an anonymous ask to GitHub for the latest version number — nothing of yours attached, and Settings can turn it off.
never
the folder itself. No backup, no cloud sync, no upload path in the code.

Want even that to stay home? Point Minne at Ollama on your own machine and model traffic never leaves it.
